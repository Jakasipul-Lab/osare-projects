# OSARE Vendor Partnership Agreement


## 1. Parties and purpose

This agreement sets the terms on which a Vendor lists its tours, stays and transport on the OSARE platform and pays OSARE a commission on the bookings OSARE brings.

- **OSARE** (EA SafariRoutes), operated by [OSARE legal name and registration number], represented by Osare Nakinson, CEO. Platform: www.easafariroutes.com.
- **The Vendor**, as named in Schedule A.

OSARE connects travelers with vendors. OSARE is not the tour operator, hotel or transport provider, and the service the traveler buys is provided by the Vendor.

## 2. Definitions

These six terms carry the same meaning wherever they appear in this agreement.

- **Platform:** the OSARE website, its search and listing pages, and OSARE's WhatsApp and social media channels.
- **Listing:** a page on the Platform describing one of the Vendor's tours, stays or transport services.
- **Traveler:** a person or group who enquires about or books a service from the Vendor.
- **Enquiry:** a request from a Traveler that reaches the Vendor through the Platform, through OSARE on WhatsApp, or by OSARE's referral.
- **Confirmed Booking:** a booking the Traveler has agreed with the Vendor and paid for, in full or with a deposit.
- **Commission:** the percentage of the booking price that the Vendor pays OSARE under section 6.

## 3. Listing and visibility levels

Listing on the Platform is free, with no upfront or monthly fee. The Vendor pays only the Commission when a Confirmed Booking happens.

OSARE shows listings in three levels, in this order:

1. **OSARE Verified:** the Vendor has confirmed its phone or WhatsApp number, location and current details in writing, and the Listing has the Vendor's own photo. These listings come first and carry the OSARE Verified seal.
2. **Listed Partner:** the Listing is on the Platform with a photo, but the Vendor has not yet confirmed its details. These listings come second, without the seal.
3. **Pending:** no confirmed details and no photo. These listings come last.

A Vendor moves up as soon as it sends the missing details; OSARE updates the level within a few working days. The seal means OSARE checked the Vendor's contact details and location on the date of verification. It does not guarantee the quality of the Vendor's service.

A Vendor may choose the optional Featured Partner level in section 6. Featured listings are always labelled "Featured" so that Travelers can see why they are shown prominently.

## 4. Vendor responsibilities

The Vendor keeps its Listing true, current and answered, because Travelers decide on what the Listing says.

- Give correct details: business name, location, phone or WhatsApp number, email and a contact person.
- Keep prices, inclusions, exclusions, dates and availability up to date, and tell OSARE within 7 days of a change.
- Hold every licence, permit and insurance that the law requires for the services offered, and show proof to OSARE on request.
- Provide photos of its own business, vehicles, rooms or tours, taken by the Vendor or used with permission. Stock photos do not count for verification.
- Reply to Enquiries from OSARE and from Travelers within 48 hours.
- Charge Travelers the price shown on the Listing, unless a Traveler and the Vendor agree a different price in writing, and report that price to OSARE.

## 5. OSARE responsibilities

OSARE brings Travelers to the Vendor and keeps the Platform honest and working.

- List the Vendor's services on the Platform free of charge.
- Promote listings through the Platform, search results and OSARE's social media, with Verified and Featured listings shown first.
- Pass Enquiries to the Vendor promptly, with the Traveler's contact details and what the Traveler asked for.
- Show the Vendor's contact details and price information as the Vendor supplied them.
- Correct or remove content on request, as set out in section 12.
- Confirm each Commission payment it receives to the Vendor on WhatsApp.

## 6. Commission

The Vendor pays OSARE 5% of the booking price on every Confirmed Booking that comes from an Enquiry, and nothing on any other booking.

| Level | Commission | When it applies |
|:--|:--|:----------------|
| Standard | 5% | Every Vendor, by default |
| Featured Partner | 10% | Only if the Vendor chooses it in writing in Schedule A or by WhatsApp or email to OSARE |

The commission is calculated on the total price the Traveler pays the Vendor for the booked services. Government fees, park fees and taxes that the Vendor collects for the authorities and shows separately are excluded [delete this sentence if not applicable].

A change of rate needs the written agreement of both sides, applies only to bookings confirmed after the change, and is never retroactive. No commission is due on bookings that did not come from an OSARE Enquiry.

## 7. Confirmed bookings and reporting

A booking counts for Commission when the Traveler first reached the Vendor through an Enquiry and then agreed and paid for the services.

- **Reporting:** the Vendor tells OSARE on WhatsApp or by email within 2 days of every Confirmed Booking that came from an Enquiry, with the Traveler's name, the service, the dates and the price.
- **Repeat and extended bookings:** a further booking by the same Traveler or travel group within 12 months of the first Enquiry counts as part of the same Enquiry.
- **Group and add-on bookings:** extra nights, upgrades and added services bought by the same Traveler for the same trip are included in the booking price.
- **Records:** the Vendor keeps booking and payment records for 12 months, and shows OSARE the records for OSARE-sourced bookings if a question arises.

## 8. Settlement of commission

The Vendor pays each Commission within 7 days after it receives the Traveler's payment, and both sides confirm the payment on WhatsApp.

- **Deposits:** the Commission on a deposit is due within 7 days of the deposit; the Commission on the balance is due within 7 days of the balance.
- **Currency:** the Commission is paid in the currency the Traveler paid, or in US dollars if both sides agree.
- **Method:** bank transfer or mobile money, as chosen in Schedule A.
- **Confirmation:** the Vendor sends OSARE the payment proof on WhatsApp (+254 758 378729). OSARE replies to confirm receipt. A payment counts as settled once OSARE has confirmed it.
- **Late payment:** if a Commission is more than 14 days overdue, OSARE may move the Vendor's Listing to the Pending level or pause it until the amount is paid.

## 9. Bookings, cancellations, refunds and safety

The contract for the service is between the Traveler and the Vendor, so the Vendor alone is responsible for delivering it safely.

- The Vendor sets its own cancellation and refund terms and shows them to the Traveler before the Traveler pays.
- The Vendor is responsible for the quality, safety, guides, vehicles, accommodation and equipment of the services it provides, and for the insurance and licences the law requires.
- If the Vendor refunds a Traveler, the Commission on the refunded amount is returned or credited to the Vendor. No Commission is due on money that is refunded.
- If the Vendor cannot provide a booked service, it tells the Traveler and OSARE at once and offers a refund or a fair alternative.

## 10. Content and intellectual property

The Vendor keeps ownership of its photos, videos and text, and allows OSARE to use them to promote the Vendor.

- The Vendor gives OSARE a free, non-exclusive right to show the content it supplies on the Platform and on OSARE's social media, for as long as the Listing is active and for a reasonable time after to allow removal.
- The Vendor confirms that it owns the content or has the right to provide it, and that the content does not mislead Travelers.
- OSARE may crop, resize or add the OSARE name or seal to supplied photos and videos, and will not change what they show.
- OSARE may remove content that is misleading, offensive or not the Vendor's, and tells the Vendor when it does.
- OSARE's name, logo and seal remain OSARE's property. The Vendor may use the OSARE Verified seal only while its Listing is at the Verified level.

## 11. Fair dealing

Both sides deal honestly, and the Vendor does not take an OSARE Enquiry off the record to avoid the Commission.

- The Vendor does not move a Traveler who came through OSARE to a private deal, a different company or a lower recorded price in order to avoid the Commission.
- The Vendor does not post fake reviews, ask others to post them, or misdescribe its services, prices or qualifications.
- OSARE uses a Traveler's contact details only to connect the Traveler with the Vendor and does not sell them to others.
- Both sides treat Travelers and each other with respect and answer messages in good time.
- If the Vendor believes a Traveler has been dishonest, it tells OSARE so that both sides can look into it.

## 12. Term and termination

This agreement starts on the date of signing and continues until either side ends it with 30 days' written notice.

- **Notice:** WhatsApp or email is enough as written notice.
- **Immediate suspension:** OSARE may suspend a Listing at once if the Vendor gives false information, acts dishonestly, or endangers Travelers.
- **Commission after the end:** Commission remains due on every booking confirmed before the end date, and on Enquiries made before the end date that become Confirmed Bookings within 6 months.
- **Removal of the Listing:** at the Vendor's request, OSARE removes the Listing within 7 days. OSARE may keep a record of Enquiries it passed on.
- **What survives:** sections 6 to 8 (Commission, reporting and settlement), 11 and 13 continue after the agreement ends.

## 13. Liability, data protection and disputes

OSARE is responsible for introducing Travelers and running the Platform, and the Vendor is responsible for the service it sells.

- **Liability:** OSARE is not liable for the Vendor's services, for Traveler claims about them, or for loss from a booking between a Traveler and the Vendor. OSARE's liability to the Vendor for any other claim is limited to the Commission OSARE received on the booking concerned.
- **No guarantee of business:** OSARE does not promise a number of Enquiries or bookings, and the Platform is provided as available.
- **Data protection:** both sides handle Travelers' personal data lawfully, use it only for the booking, and protect it from misuse.
- **No partnership or employment:** the Vendor and OSARE are independent businesses, and neither can bind the other.
- **Disputes:** both sides first try to settle a problem by talking, on WhatsApp or by phone. If that fails, they try mediation by an agreed neutral person before going to court.
- **Governing law:** the laws of [country to be agreed, for example Kenya or Tanzania] apply to this agreement.
- **Whole agreement:** this agreement and Schedule A are the whole agreement. A change counts only if both sides confirm it in writing.
